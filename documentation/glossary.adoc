== Glossary

This chapter provides project-specific terms for the eCMR.

The project uses the eCMR terminology as described in the UN/CEFACT standard.

Source: UN/CEFACT, document "Business Requirements Specification eCMR", Chapter 6.2 "Definitions Business Terms" (https://unece.org/trade/uncefact/mainstandards[Link])

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Definition

| Audit Trail
| A verifiable record of all modifications and exchanges of an eCMR. In the eCMR system, the audit trail is derived from the eSeal chain.

| Canonical Transferable Unit
| The official representation of the eCMR that is transmitted between partners. In this system, the eSeal serves as the canonical transferable unit instead of the raw eCMR.

| Carrier / Subsequent Carrier
| The party providing transport services as stipulated in a contract of carriage by road (eCMR).

| Consignment
| A separately identifiable collection of Consignment Items to be transported from one consignor to one consignee under a single transport contract (eCMR).

| Consignment Item
| A separately identifiable quantity of products grouped by customs tariff code or packaging for transport. Each Consignment Item must have only one associated customs tariff code in cross-border shipments to satisfy customs requirements. It represents the lowest level of information within a consignment.

| Contract of Carriage by Road (eCMR)
| A contract in which a transport company, against payment of freight, undertakes to carry goods from one place to another. In the CMR Convention, a contract of carriage is confirmed by the issuance of a consignment note.

| Delivery Party
| The party to which goods should be delivered by the carrier. Operational terms: "Place of Positioning" or "Ship-to".

| Dispatch Place
| The location where goods are collected or taken over by the carrier. Operational terms: "Pick-up Place" or "Ship-from".

| Digital Signature
| A cryptographic signature applied to an eCMR or eSeal to ensure integrity, authenticity, and legal recognition under the EU eIDAS Regulation.

| eCMR
| Electronic consignment note used to digitally record a contract of carriage by road, replacing the traditional paper CMR.

| eSeal (Electronic Seal)
| A cryptographic mechanism used to sign eCMRs digitally. An eSeal ensures authenticity, integrity, and non-repudiation of a document and serves as the canonical transferable unit between partners.

| eSeal Chain
| A linked sequence of eSeals, where each new eSeal references its predecessor. This chain enables reconstruction of the full history of an eCMR and ensures a complete audit trail of all changes.

| eSeal Header
| Metadata accompanying an eSeal, including the public key of the issuer and other relevant verification information.

| Interoperability
| The ability of independent eCMR instances to exchange and verify eSeals, ensuring legally compliant and trusted communication across organizational boundaries.

| Public Key Verification
| The process of validating the public key used in an eSeal. The checksum of the public key is published via a DNS record of the providing eCMR instance to enable secure verification.

| Sender / Consignor
| The party consigning goods under a contract of carriage by road (eCMR), referred to as "Sender" in the CMR Convention.

| Consignee
| The party receiving a consignment of goods as stipulated in a contract of carriage by road (eCMR).

| Transport Service Contract
| A contract between a transport services buyer and provider specifying the transport of goods.

| Transport Services Buyer (Sender/Consignor or Consignee)
| The party purchasing transport services.

| Transport Services Provider (Carrier)
| The party providing transport services.

| TDED / Trade Data Elements Directory
| The United Nations Trade Data Elements Directory (UNTDED, ISO 7372) provides a standardized repository for the semantics of trade data elements used in international trade. Each data element has a four-digit identifier, a name, and a descriptive definition. https://unece.org/DAM/trade/untdid/UNTDED2005.pdf

| Trust Service Provider (TSP)
| A service provider authorized to issue digital certificates or provide verification services that support legally recognized digital signatures and eSeals in compliance with eIDAS.
|===
